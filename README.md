# Signal-Zero
Signal Zero is an top-down perspective shooter game with arcade mechanics but still having an story to follow.
## The story
The game follows an story of Gary Knox living in the year 3012, every country has merged into one singular nation named "The United Nations". The United Nations launch an new thing, robots! They are supposed to help people with everyday stuff, teach different things and mostly keep the crime rate low. The robots take the order too literally and they put EVERY human in their system to jail or other places to be held against their will, or atleast they try to, the main character Gary Knox gets called but he is not an big fan of the robots so he shoots the robot tasked to take him. Then Gary makes it his mission to free people and destroy the AI servers.
